Our Journey

From One Cart to a City Favorite

A short walk through the milestones that shaped Cho'ice~World into what it is today.

2016

The First Cart

Started as a single pushcart outside a college campus, serving three flavours churned the night before.

2018

Our First Store

Opened our first proper storefront in Banjara Hills, growing the menu from 3 flavours to over 20.

2020

Milkshakes Launch

Introduced our signature milkshake line, quickly becoming half of everything we sell.

2023

50+ Flavours

Crossed 50 signature flavours and opened two more locations across the city.

2026

8 Years & Counting

Now serving thousands of happy customers a month, with the same handmade promise we started with.
