1. Introduction

Cho'ice~World ("we", "us", or "our") operates this website and our in-store ordering systems. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, place an order, or otherwise interact with us. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information collected automatically as you use our services, and information from third parties.

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and password when you register.
  • Order information — items purchased, delivery address, and payment details (processed securely by our payment provider).
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on the site, and general browsing behaviour.
  • Device data — IP address, browser type, and device identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Create and manage your account
  • Send order confirmations, receipts, and support messages
  • Improve our website, menu, and overall customer experience
  • Send promotional emails, where you've opted in, about new flavours and offers
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse of our services

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences (like dark mode or language direction), and understand how our site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect some site functionality.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Delivery partners, to fulfil your order
  • Payment processors, to securely handle transactions
  • Service providers who support our website, email, and analytics
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us directly.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — including encryption in transit and restricted access controls — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
